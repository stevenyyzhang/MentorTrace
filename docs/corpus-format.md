# English public corpus interface

New ordinary v1.2 runs use `direct_fulltext_adaptive_budget_v1`: no preliminary Advisor task map or initial task-based source selection. Complete inventories and active links feed packing directly. Freeze `config/advisor-workload.json` at creation and apply its compact workload profile alongside unchanged capacity reserves. Minimize complete packets within those bounds, preferring at most 12 and automatically allowing more when needed without planning confirmation. Freeze `max(12, actual planned packets)` submission slots; no paid retries are automatic. Expression count has no separate hard cap; operations and complexity remain bounded. Check complete-request capacity and workload before submission. Reader/Expert keep their object plans, isolated from Advisor. Existing frozen direct/task-map protocols remain reproducible. See [Advisor batching](../.agents/skills/mentortrace/references/advisor-batching.md) for the full rules.

All corpus field names and analytical content are English. The final author report uses English followed by Chinese according to the skill's report-language contract.

Each concern has an anonymous `id`, six decision objects, and `evidence_status`. The decision objects are `core_question`, `new_manuscript_check`, `relationship_to_check`, `historically_insufficient_response`, `potentially_sufficient_response`, and `applicability_boundary`. Each contains `text`, `basis`, and anonymous `source_ids`. A source ID is a linkage token, not a public document title, filesystem location or proof of a current defect.

`evidence_status` distinguishes a public analyst abstraction from private historical evidence and advisor acceptance. Private histories are unavailable in this distribution. Explicitly record missing evidence and do not infer that an adequate proposed response was historically implemented or accepted. The six fields must be read together; a short question without its conditions is insufficient.

Private source records previously held annotation text (`original_comments`), the manuscript text marked by an annotation (`marked_text`), neighboring text (`local_context`), and version/transition quotations. Those records and the private identity mapping are excluded from public corpora. Generalized analytical statements replace source quotations. They are not presented as verbatim mentor instructions or certified universal requirements.

Published cases carry `public_source` containing the public title, DOI, URL and year. These are public bibliographic references, not bundled local PDFs. `full_case` and `full_source_record` mean the complete analytical summary record available in this release, not a copy of the original article. Expert summaries are not independent re-proofs. The indexes and frozen route link cases to actual target tasks; case IDs never substitute for evidence from the target manuscript.

Reader records omit undocumented `plausible_alternative`. The former `actual_reader_benefit` is `hypothesized_reader_benefit`; no reader-study outcome is implied. Expert `sufficient_alternative` is removed; an optional `transfer_hypothesis` is an analyst research note and `assessed_target_sufficiency` is null. `analysis_status` separates reconstruction, effects and transfer evidence. Unvalidated editing recipes and analyst critiques are excluded from runtime selected cases and revision-proposal requests. Expert execution views supply the source-specific description and evidence limits. Routes require a `sufficiency_question`, not a manufactured sufficient alternative.

The 11 Reader categories group source examples from 2–4 papers each; the 14 Expert categories nominate cases from 4–11 papers each. These overlapping, curated groups are recurring task relationships, not a survey showing common strengths of every paper or superior writing. Expert `case_role` distinguishes source task examples, source scope-boundary examples and mixed source/analyst notes. See `case-quality-audit.md` and the corpus `category_evidence.json` for support and limits.

The frozen package manifest and FREEZE.json must be regenerated together when content changes. Frozen corpus paths disable Git text normalization so checkout preserves the exact bytes bound by FREEZE.json; other text files use LF. Runtime batches must preserve full fields, card order and unique coverage. Do not reuse hashes, manifests or batch counts from a different package.

Public abstractions and withheld history do not establish diagnostic accuracy. Any quality comparison must control manuscript inputs, model settings and generic review rules separately from corpus changes.

The current default is `advisor-concerns-v4`: 493 cards stored in 17 unchanged complete-field archive batches, comprising the unchanged 437-card baseline and 56 added conditional abstractions. v1.2 adds `config/advisor-groups.json` with 12 fixed ownership groups for all 493 Concerns and 1,581 Expressions. New ordinary reviews retain these groups as provenance, cluster shared Concern context across groups and pack to complete-request capacity with unchanged reserves. Primary inspection is assigned exactly once near related Expressions when available; neither the archive count nor a target of 20 determines the runtime call count. All 271 unlinked Expressions are included. The source corpora themselves are unchanged from v1.1.0. Earlier Concern snapshots remain in historical releases or local archives for frozen runs and comparisons. The additional collection is marked as training-exposed through an anonymous group and a matching promotion registry. Its source identities, version chains and reuse/merge/hold decisions are private. Versions and related research extensions must not be treated as independent evaluation samples; public linkage alone cannot establish held-out eligibility.

`config/expression-concern-links.json` independently records the reassessment of all 2,585 existing candidate links against final retained Expression content and complete Concern cards. A fresh review records the actual model settings, evidence quotations, reasons, applicability conditions and call hashes. This is a relevance review of existing links, not a search of every possible Expression–Concern pair or a repeat of source admission. Direct and conditional links remain active; uncertain links remain active with their unresolved conditions. Only unsupported links marked `historical` leave required source-use accounting. That legacy label does not certify historical events. Every original source record and link remains recoverable, and every Expression still receives independent manuscript review. Conditional notes supplement the complete evidence and never establish a manuscript defect.
