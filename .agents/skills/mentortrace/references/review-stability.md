# Stable coverage across independent reviews

Different valid wording and new findings are expected; loss of a supported requirement
needs a disposition. More comments cannot compensate for a missed requirement. This
contract reduces omissions and controls final delivery; it does not promise identical
independent model outputs or complete discovery.

## Within the independent run

Reader and Expert follow manuscript objects through the evidence needed for their
actual claims. For experiments/figures, distinguish input scale and reference,
processing, plotted quantity, statistical basis and interpretation. A sufficient
answer about one does not cover the others. Empirical statistics may need fixed and
resampled quantities and variability; analytical values need assumptions and a
calculation, without an invented experiment. Reader also checks the precise relation
between contributions in literature summaries. Missing cited evidence leaves a
specific anchored question, not a generic literature disclaimer.

Use existing checks, technical_scope, Reader reading_review and the source-only scope
review. The source reviewer checks whether actual manuscript objects or dimensions
were left outside the declared scope, including checks that never became findings.
Supported additions go through Verify with their real provenance; absent review is
not itself a manuscript defect. Do not add a second complete review or a fixed defect
quota. These questions never become an Advisor task map or imported verdicts.

Merge and source-requirement review separate independently answerable requirements.
Retain object, condition, requested fact/action and occurrence in existing components.
Shared topics, titles, source IDs and a broad request to "clarify the method" do not
establish preservation. In Verify and delivery ask whether an author could follow the
surviving action fully while leaving a particular supported question unanswered.
Record unsupported clauses and adequate equivalents with manuscript evidence.

## After independent freeze, when history is in scope

1. Preserve the independent report and its discovery provenance. Only then open prior
   reports. Inventory the complete previous report, including unnumbered remaining
   checks and substantive conditions in shared actions; split compound requirements.
2. Judge old requirements against the current manuscript. Record exact old excerpts,
   manuscript evidence, current report excerpts and covered/rejected_with_evidence/
   confirmed_omission/unresolved. Optional suggestions retain their optional status;
   historical presence alone cannot establish correctness or require an incompatible edit.
3. Resolve confirmed supported omissions in a separate delivery revision or an explicit
   integrated addendum, with concrete actions in the relevant comment category. A quality
   comparison that only lists the loss is not a completed replacement report. A genuine
   unknown stays a precise author-confirmation request. Do not label recovery as fresh
   independent discovery. If the user requested comparison only, report the loss without
   silently expanding the task into a replacement report.
4. Bind the earlier delivered report as an additional source in the existing preservation
   ledger. Review changed clauses, bilingual counterparts and affected dependencies; reuse
   valid unchanged judgments with their provenance. A supported required omission blocks
   claiming that the new delivery replaces the earlier one. An optional exclusion needs
   the existing editorial disposition, not a technical-error claim.

Keep a short difference record: new discovery, recovered omission, supported withdrawal,
unresolved question and presentation-only change. This supports quality comparison
without equating comment count with recall. Never force two blind runs to agree by
feeding one report into the other's initial review.


For new `delivery_reliability: 1` runs, apply [delivery reliability](delivery-reliability.md). Its explicit candidate/requirement decisions and local-validity recovery extend this contract; historical frozen runs retain the rules above. Local reuse does not imply whole-report approval, and style alignment does not imply improved quality.


For new `delivery_hardening: 1` runs, the additional [delivery hardening contract](delivery-hardening.md) governs source/component retention, actual-result barriers and scoped recovery. Older frozen protocols retain their existing rules.
