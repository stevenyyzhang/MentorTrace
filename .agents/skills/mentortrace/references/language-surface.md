# Full-text language and surface QA

Apply source-fidelity.md and separately dispose of every supplied source candidate. A page-level checked status does not account for an inline split-word candidate. Inspect its physical location on the page to distinguish an unintended hyphen/space, normal line-end division, a legitimate compound and extraction corruption. Preserve raw text; do not silently join words before checking. These candidates supplement the complete language read and do not enumerate every possible spelling or typography problem.

Inspect every supplied text block and all corresponding page-image content, including captions, tables, equations, headings and references. This lane is independent of object planning and advisor cards. Coverage records certify processing accounting, not zero missed errors; unreadable text stays unreadable and unprocessed text stays unfinished.

Check spelling and obvious typos; grammar (agreement, articles, number, tense and prepositions); incomplete or malformed sentences; notation consistency; cross-reference targets; and formatting conventions. Record definite small errors regardless of technical importance. Valid but awkward prose belongs in optional_suggestions, not the error list. Verify broken extraction, ligatures and line-end hyphens against page images before attributing an error to the author.

Use two complementary reads of each page. First read the prose in manuscript order, reconstructing sentences across line breaks, columns and page boundaries with the page image as the authority. Then make a focused sentence-level sweep: check what each modifier and relative clause refers to; subject–verb agreement and number; determiners; verb patterns and preposition collocations; parallel actions or lists; and tense or aspect where it changes the intended time relation. Inspect section titles, captions, figure/table labels, equation-adjacent prose and references separately so short text is not skipped. Compare repeated method names and defined terms across occurrences. These are prompts to inspect the actual sentence, not patterns that automatically prove an error. A text unit is `checked` only after both reads and its applicable display items have been attempted; otherwise mark the unfinished or unreadable scope in coverage. In the coverage reason, state the attempted scope rather than merely saying the page was checked.

Resolve each candidate against its full sentence and nearby technical context. Put a definite local error in findings even when its correction is small; put a grammatical but merely less natural phrasing in optional_suggestions; put an author-dependent term, symbol, attribution or substantive rewrite in uncertain_items with a precise check request. A replacement that changes a method, result or claim needs the same evidence check as a content revision. Do not turn a style preference, a possible alternative tense, or a PDF extraction artifact into a confirmed typo. The sweep has no target finding count and does not require listing clean sentences.

Use one anchored finding per independently correctable error. Give the observed text, proposed correction, and brief basis. Preserve all occurrence locations when an error repeats. Suggested text is a proposal only: never change the manuscript. Do not normalize unfamiliar terminology, variable names or mathematical expressions merely for consistency. When the correct symbol, dimension, citation support or intended technical meaning is uncertain, record the concrete question in uncertain_items for professional review; do not guess a replacement.

At merge and verification retain finding_type=surface, category and suggested_text, preserving occurrence anchors. Do not merge a surface correction into a technical finding. Verify linguistic correctness and source evidence, not technical significance: smallness alone is never a withdrawal reason. Withdraw false alarms with anchored evidence; preserve uncertainty and optional suggestions separately. Surface counts must not enter advisor-core hit scores.

For a later review of the same manuscript, keep earlier delivered language comments out of this lane's independent discovery. After the new raw findings are frozen, compare each earlier language correction with the current manuscript and proposed report. Retain a still-applicable correction or record an anchored reason for narrowing, withdrawing or leaving it unresolved; silence in the new Language response is not a disposition. This comparison is delivery preservation, not a new independent discovery or an instruction to copy old wording.
