# Reader retrieval from published-case summaries

The public library contains English analyst-written summaries with public citations. It contains no original selected paragraphs or neighboring page transcriptions. Consult its manifest for case counts; navigation entries are tasks, not requirements for every manuscript.

An explicitly selected private case-passage snapshot may additionally supply actual
original anchor and neighboring page text under `source_passages`. Read it in context
when borrowing sentence roles, information selection or wording; distinguish it from
the analyst account. A source's actual prose is not mentor-authored evidence. Preserve
its extraction/reading limits and use target facts for any newly generated replacement.

Route after identifying an actual target reader task and comprehension dependency. Match the reader task, reconstructed relation and applicability boundary. A topic or chapter label alone is insufficient. A route records a `sufficiency_question`, without declaring a verdict or manufacturing a reasonable alternative. Read the selected summary and its `analysis_status`; do not pretend the omitted source paragraph was read. See case-evidence.md for evidence classes.

Judge the current manuscript from its own sentences, figures and legitimate forward references. Formulas, tables, captions and nearby prose may already provide sufficient information. Separate necessary explanation from optional preference. A summary can sharpen a check or recognize sufficiency; it need not create a finding. Zero matches remain valid and generic checks continue.

Undocumented `plausible_alternative` values are withheld. A proposed alternative is optional and must preserve the target's necessary information and dependencies before it can be called sufficient or preferable. Delaying a prerequisite to create an inferior comparison does not validate a source order. Reader benefits are hypotheses, not measured effects.

After findings freeze, develop wording from verified target requirements, facts and boundaries. The runtime excludes unvalidated writing recipes; the proposal request supplies relation references rather than source templates. Do not import source numbers, technical claims, assumptions or a mandatory source order. Missing target facts require precise author questions. Case IDs belong in case_usage and provenance; they do not prove a defect and are not Advisor card IDs.

## Editorial reading through cases

Reader uses cases for organization and flow as well as explanatory completeness.
Match cases to actual editorial tasks in reader_editorial_plan: information selection,
relative emphasis, sentence/paragraph roles, handoffs, and independent reading of
abstracts, captions or conclusions. Read the complete supplied case account, compare
its task and conditions with this manuscript, then ask whether retaining, compressing,
moving or rewriting a passage would serve the target's purpose. A case's prose/order
is an example to consider, not a mandatory target format or evidence of a defect.

Use existing case_usage and writing_transfers to record the learned distinction,
target anchors, kept dependencies and resulting check or optional suggestion. Necessary
explanations stay in content findings; applicable optional organization proposals go to
organization_suggestions. The four coverage axes in review_quality complement this
case-based reading and do not replace it. Do not force a case match per axis or dismiss
an otherwise supported proposal merely because its comparative benefit is uncertain.
Advisor independently uses its own Concern/Expression context; this Reader routing is
not shared as an Advisor checklist.
