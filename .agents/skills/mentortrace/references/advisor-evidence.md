# Conditional advisor evidence

Use supplied card decision fields, including evidence status and applicability limits. Do not replace cards with titles or double-count overlapping archives. Follow the frozen family policy: held-out review excludes target-family evidence; explicitly authorized development inclusion must be disclosed. Grouping is navigation, not grounds to discard variants.

## Independent discovery before alignment

Receive the full manuscript and images plus the current card batch, without the general plan, earlier opinions or other batches' diagnoses. First identify an evidenced distinction in a card; then search the manuscript for corresponding roles and relationships; only then judge current support. Where documented, use why a historical answer did not resolve the concern. Do not invent a historical failure to fill a field.

Declare independently answerable targets as local `relation_id` records with anchors, relationships and passage responsibilities. Checks reference these targets instead of freely rewriting them. A justified scope reinterpretation belongs in the evidence/reason record, not a silent replacement of the target. Independent manuscript-driven findings are allowed; no quota of new objects or criticisms applies.

For each card record `linked`, `not_applicable`, `unknown` or `unfinished`, local relation IDs and a brief reason. Linked is association, not a sufficient/completed verdict. One card may support several relations or none. Every declared relation has a completed or unfinished check. Finding no counterpart is not automatically a structural defect in the manuscript.

Freeze the response before matching it to general-plan objects or other opinions. Preserve unmatched relations; do not require them to fit the original plan. A runtime adapter may represent local relations as objects for compatibility while retaining the original output and explicit ID mapping.

For an applied card locate the new manuscript's relevant relation, migration conditions, existing explanation and strongest contrary evidence. A different explanation may close the concern; a historical student revision is not proof of correctness or advisor acceptance.

Initial batches cannot read each other's diagnoses or target feedback. Requested historical evidence enters logged follow-up calls, distinct from initial independent discovery. Integration uses supplied findings/support; card IDs are provenance, not proof of a current defect. Loading and citing cards do not prove causal contribution; controlled comparison is needed.

When a card or manuscript passage motivates a concrete change in sentence/paragraph order, distinguish whole-paper support from accessibility where the information is needed. Preserve the source and proposed destination, reader benefit, affected references/transitions and author-dependent conditions. A necessary comprehension repair belongs in a content finding; a useful optional rearrangement belongs in `organization_suggestions` when that runtime field is supplied, even if the content check is sufficient. Do not force the proposal into a defect or bury it only in the check's reason. This does not require Advisor to use the general plan.

## Apply a conditional card

Read its core question, transfer cue, relationship, insufficient historical response, potentially sufficient response and boundary together. A cue is a search aid, not a necessary keyword: a relationship about unavailable detector inputs can also apply to another algorithm consuming unavailable state. Conversely, shared terminology does not establish applicability.

Trigger a check from the current research claim, operation or dependency, not from the prior existence of the explanation being checked. A missing definition, account of information flow, evidence or summary component can be the result of the check; it is not by itself grounds for `not_applicable`. Historical counts, notation and presentation locations are examples unless essential to the relationship. Preserve necessary technical assumptions: missing evidence may require `unknown`, and a different research dependency may truly be inapplicable. Distinguish applicability, sufficiency, a supported gap and inability to judge; absence alone does not prove a defect.

Locate a concrete current object and ask what the current manuscript already provides. Test the remaining requirement against that explanation. Prefer a correct alternative explanation over literal imitation of a historical student revision. Preserve the distinction between a mentor's explicit requirement, the student's change, and an analyst's proposed sufficiency criterion.

If no current object matches, give a brief applicability reason. Private version histories are absent from public cards. Record uncertainty rather than requesting nonexistent history. Only a separately supplied package that explicitly declares historical fields available can support a specific logged follow-up. This does not supply unknown facts about the new manuscript: those remain author questions.

Do not send titles as a replacement for decision fields. Batch count and size follow the frozen capacity plan; process every supplied card against the full manuscript. The frozen public manifest defines the available cards and batches for a run. Card dispositions are coverage accounting, not proof of successful reasoning. Report budget limits rather than truncating history or labeling unseen evidence irrelevant.

For section-level argument, information selection and whole-paper summary checks, also follow [section-argument.md](section-argument.md). Its runtime module is enabled for new section-review protocols; frozen older requests are unchanged.

## Public abstraction status

The public corpus preserves English analytical distinctions and anonymous links, not original mentor quotations. No advisor acceptance may be inferred from publication or anonymous source IDs. Treat the provided evidence_status limitations as operative; the distinction between analyst inference and verified historical evidence must survive diagnosis and delivery.
