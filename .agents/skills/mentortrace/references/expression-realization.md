# Concrete mentor language in replacement passages

Use with an Expression snapshot declaring `expression_realization: 1`, after the
revision scope is fixed. Keep Concern, Expression and published examples distinct.
This extends existing drafting and final-text review, not the four discovery lanes.

For `public_minimized_expression_v1` sources, read the complete supplied minimized
examples and conditional analysis. Original pages and private locators are absent;
do not claim to have checked them or infer their omitted content. `source_ref`
binds a public example ID and `source_quote` binds its exact retained text.
For schema-version 2 analytical operations, the reference instead binds the
record's supplied operation account. Its quote is analysis, not mentor text.
Use `evidence_kind: analytical_operation` and `realization_type: editorial_operation`
only; the omitted comment cannot be reconstructed or learned as body wording.
An actual mentor title/heading fragment with `body_form_allowed: false` retains
its literal source genre but supports `editorial_operation` only. Its body frame
is withheld. This permits the specific title edit without promoting it to a
body-sentence model; generated examples and comments do not gain that permission.
The adapter admits actual body proposals, deletion operations and bounded paragraph
instructions separately. Purely generated examples or unsupported positive language
features cannot establish realized mentor style. Existing genre, target-fit,
meaning and actual-replacement checks apply to both private and public sources.

Read the exact mentor proposal or attributed deletion in its complete context.
Learn concrete subject/verb/complement constructions, collocations, qualification,
transitions and sentence progression. A direction to an author is an editorial
operation, not manuscript prose. A deleted author word is not mentor-authored text.
An analytical sentence frame is not a quotation or a universal preference. Existing
unclassified language choices need genre and target-fit judgments before use.
Held operations remain ineligible, including when embedded in a longer proposal.

Ordinary comments cannot be learned as manuscript wording or syntax, even when
they contain fluent, complete English sentences. Require explicit body insertion
or replacement evidence: a contextualized insertion marker, a reply-linked
replacement, or a directly paired before/replacement span. Metadata proposes a
candidate; it does not automatically admit instructions as body prose. Keep
`body_evidence` and `realization_type` separate: `language_form` requires actual
proposed body text, `language_edit` describes a supported deletion, and
`editorial_operation` uses comments or actual non-body mentor examples only for
the bounded modification action, preserving their distinct evidence roles.

For each retained drafting task, retrieve by passage job and language function,
then judge semantic compatibility. Additional post-freeze language retrieval may
guide wording only; record its timing and never turn it into an earlier discovery.
Read admitted companions with their original conditions. Topic vocabulary alone
does not establish fit. No match is valid and must not become invented attribution.

Fix target facts, equations, terms, citations, qualification and agreed editing
scope before choosing forms. Identify the exact source-supported feature to retain
or adapt in the replacement. For example, a task-centered `The task ... is to ...`
construction can transfer while `first ... then ...` cannot unless the target
actually establishes that sequence. Preserve the concrete linguistic feature when
it fits; a general claim about 'clarity' or 'task before difficulty' does not certify
sentence-form learning. Do not force a paragraph rewrite when an exact move,
deletion or paragraph break implements the agreed action.

Return the versioned `language_realizations` contract from
`scripts/expression_realization.py`. Bind every used feature to the actual English
replacement span, separately from review commentary. Multiple source features may
support one coordinated paragraph. Record rejected candidates and adaptations;
do not infer mentor acceptance, add findings or expand the accepted editing scope.

Extend the existing final-text factual/meaning/style review with `realization_review`.
The reviewer inspects the source genre, target fit, realized span and full paragraph,
not just the drafter's attribution. Bind the response and writing evidence hashes
in the delivery ledger's `language_realization` field. Use
`audit_report_preservation.py init --language-response RESPONSE --language-evidence EVIDENCE`
with the normal report/source arguments, then complete its pending review together
with the ordinary final-text review. A rejected feature or changed replacement
invalidates the affected review. Mechanical checks establish records and hashes;
only contextual review assesses meaning and naturalness. Neither certifies the
mentor's preference for the new manuscript.

Private coverage distinguishes reused existing language choices, pilot-enriched
features, operation-only records and held choices. Full inventory coverage does
not mean that every record supplies a sentence template. Normal author reports
show the issue and usable passage; private source details appear only in an
explicitly requested learning/comparison report.
