# Advisor Expression evidence

Normal v1.1.0 CLI reviews use the bundled `corpus/advisor-expression-v1` for Advisor and drafting. Use `--expression PATH` only for an explicit override, or `--no-expression` for a baseline. Private configuration must not silently replace this default.

A minimized public snapshot has role `public_advisor_expression` or the local
`public_expression_candidate` role. The runner validates its anonymous record
schema and file inventory; original episodes, images and mappings are absent.
Its runtime records declare `source_format: public_minimized_expression_v1`.
Schema-version 2 packages omit literal editorial instructions. An admitted
paragraph/sentence-order feature may bind `evidence_account` to an exact supplied
analytical operation instead of an example. The runtime labels it
`analytical_operation`; it supports an editorial action, never mentor wording.
Actual mentor title or heading examples marked `body_form_allowed: false` can
retain a concrete conditional editing operation. Their body-drafting frames are
withheld; do not turn the title example into a positive body sentence pattern.
Read all supplied examples, analytical accounts, conditions and limits rather
than inventing the omitted original context. A candidate is usable for local
checks; loading it does not approve publication or establish rewriting quality.

For public records, quote a continuous exact span from the explicitly identified
example or analytical field, using `expression_excerpt_basis` in transfers and
draft attribution. Distinguish actual mentor wording, editorial instructions,
author-before/deletion text and generated examples. An operation-only abstraction
can guide an editorial decision but cannot certify imitation of mentor prose.
Body language features bind public example IDs; no synthetic private annotation
IDs or source episodes are reconstructed. Comments remain action evidence,
deletions remain author text, and generated frames are analyst constructions.
For instruction-free v2 packages, attribute their derived operations with
`expression_excerpt_basis:{kind:"analytical_account",field:"operations"}`.
Do not describe the analytical quote as the omitted mentor comment.

Enhanced snapshots carry source-bound `language_features`, retaining exact mentor
spans separately from analytical frames and reused choices awaiting source-genre
classification. Use [expression-realization.md](expression-realization.md) for
post-freeze wording retrieval, concrete target-sentence realization and the final
language check. Literal source matching alone does not admit an editorial instruction
as replacement prose. Keep source-specific facts and held operations out of drafts.

## Expression admission and Concern overlap

For historical intake, publication review and runtime transfer, assess the learned
feature separately from quotation accuracy. Name the observable change, its
source-supported passage function, transfer conditions and limits. Determine what
it contributes beyond any applicable Concern; a title, purpose summary or generic
claim about clarity is not sufficient admission evidence.

- **Wording or sentence form:** identify an actual proposed lexical choice,
  subject/predicate/complement construction, qualification or transition and its
  contextual use. A common phrase can support a local example, but cannot alone
  establish a distinctive or general mentor preference.
- **Paragraph operation:** identify a concrete supported split, move, compression,
  role change or cross-sentence sequence. An anchored instruction can support the
  operation without supplying replacement prose. A request to explain a missing
  reason or support a claim does not by itself establish such an operation.
- **Concern without added Expression:** when the available evidence supplies only
  a substantive requirement already captured by a Concern, use that Concern.
  Do not create a second writing requirement by relabeling it as Expression.
- **Unresolved:** retain the source privately when context, attribution or the
  claimed presentation change is insufficient. Do not promote an analyst's
  plausible rewrite into historical mentor evidence.

Compare prospective Concern matches across their actual question, relationship,
response and applicability boundary. Record verified overlap separately from
possible overlap and missing review. An empty `concern_ids` list does not establish
that no Concern covers the requirement; a shared keyword does not prove coverage.
If both views are useful, retain the Concern for the requirement and the Expression
for its additional realization, with one compatible target recommendation. Missing
Concern coverage warrants a separately supported intake candidate, not an invented
Concern ID or an automatic promotion.

During explicit library intake or maintenance, record admission evidence, feature
type, concrete added value, matched Concern IDs and review disposition privately.
An ordinary manuscript review uses the frozen source records: assess target fit
and the additional operation, without repeating a library-wide admission audit. Distinguish source-bound wording examples from conditional operations
and from repeated requirements when reporting library coverage. Existing source
and schema checks do not certify this semantic review; preserve frozen snapshots
and report unreviewed cases instead of retrospectively relabeling them as passed.

An Expression match does not require a rewrite. Compare the target's current wording and arrangement with the source-inspired candidate, including preserved meaning, prerequisites, local transitions and space. For an improvement goal, compare the claimed gain with its cost. For a mentor-style writing goal, equivalent wording may be adopted when an actual source-supported feature fits the target and preserves meaning. Record style alignment as the reason; improved quality may remain unestablished. Do not require a defect or invent a benefit to justify imitation. Exact mentor wording remains source evidence; generated target wording is a new model draft, not a mentor-approved correction. Several examples supporting one editorial decision do not establish several independent defects. Carry only the preferred compatible operation into a suggested revision; record unused alternatives in the private trace.

For a private snapshot, use an explicitly selected frozen source to learn from actual
mentor wording and concrete paragraph actions. Each record retains attributed
annotation text, original page context, the observed operation, transferable task,
conditions, exceptions and links to zero or more Concerns. A proposed insertion is
not an applied revised paragraph; unknown revision authorship stays unknown.

Some conditional analyses name a companion from the same source-learning call.
`local_reference_bindings` maps that local alias to its admitted Expression ID and
original output hash. Read admitted companions with the selected record; a held
companion remains unresolved context and is never promoted into guidance. Preserve
the literal quotation and do not treat the binding as independent preference evidence.

An attributed blank deletion may retain `marked_texts` with the exact original
manuscript span and action `delete`, alongside its empty literal annotation.
This is text deleted by the mentor, not text written by the mentor. Inspect its
paragraph role on the original image; geometry alone cannot establish an edit
pair or a preference for brevity. Unexplained highlights, empty insertions and
ambiguous deletion spans remain held or have no Expression. Transfer and draft
excerpts may quote an admitted marked span with this separate attribution.

For long proposals, read the complete annotation and its paragraph context. Keep
directly proposed sentences, mentor discussion, outline headings and unfinished
placeholders distinguishable. Candidate later-version passages show an observed
response only: quote them as mentor wording only when their authorship is separately
confirmed. Preserve changed technical claims and omissions instead of treating a
matching later draft as acceptance of the whole proposal.

Expression complements Concern. In new ordinary v1.2 runs, every Expression enters
direct full-manuscript review, with or without a Concern link. No preliminary task
map or lexical retrieval selects initial sources. Find possible uses independently
from each source's operations and boundaries; associated Concerns supplement but
do not restrict this search. Reader/Expert plans and findings are not supplied.
Keep explicit nonmatch/keep/unknown/unfinished outcomes and each active source use.
Historical frozen task-map runs retain their own task dispositions and retrieval.

In `advisor_role_division: 1` runs, Expression's main contribution is concrete
realization: lexical/syntactic choice, qualification, transition or a supported
sentence/paragraph operation. Explain its added value beyond the Concern's
requirement in the existing transfer fields, and identify adopted feature IDs.
Independently seek suitable passages, but do not generate another generic rule or
defect merely because an example is present. Distinguish necessary repair
realization, mentor-style alignment without a superiority claim, optional improvement,
actual local expression error, no change and unresolved inspection. A sufficient Concern may coexist with optional expression
improvement; a compatible target can remain unchanged. Full source and active-use
coverage still applies in one call, without a preliminary applicability filter.

Read the actual source passage and mentor proposal, then identify specific changes
in information selection, sentence roles, emphasis, dependencies, transitions,
subject/verb choices and qualifications. Compare these with the target's actual
passage job. State which operations transfer, which do not, and which facts or
conditions must be preserved. Preserve complete argument context for paragraph
repairs; a one-word suggestion cannot substitute for a supported structural action.
In the v2 source view, `language_choices.proposal_source_match` distinguishes an
exact mentor excerpt, an analytical representation that is not verbatim, and an
unsupplied wording choice. Only the original `mentor_texts` establish actual mentor
wording. Literal matching alone does not make a discussion, heading or fragment an
accepted replacement passage. These labels do not alter the frozen source records.

Return the Expression writing transfers and, for legacy mapped runs, task dispositions specified
in the request. Keep appropriate target arrangements; do not require a defect per
example or rewrite every paragraph. Optional improvements need evidence of a useful
target-specific change, not a claim that the current prose is grammatically wrong.
Keep the transfer record for each linked editorial Concern as well as each selected
Expression. A shared idea does not make their source identities interchangeable.
In legacy mapped runs, link a checked task to inspection of its own target passage;
nearby-page support alone does not complete that task.
Only the selected Expression records are admitted transfer candidates. Other
annotations retained in complete original-page context remain contextual evidence;
do not promote those comments, held proposals or unselected records into new
guidance or invent a source ID for them.
Scientific assertions inside historical proposals remain source-specific and may
need qualification; mentor attribution does not certify mathematical correctness.
Only admitted operations are transfer candidates. A record's `held_operations`
preserve incomplete references, unresolved claims or other pending actions for
traceability; they cannot justify a new requirement or replacement text. Read a
later `version_response_review` alongside the original learning account, retaining
its separate reviewer and limits.

After merge/verify, carry only sources connected to retained requirements into
drafting, recursively preserving explicit admitted `local_reference_bindings`
companions and their complete episodes. Record an actual companion ID when its
operation contributes; an explicit companion remains bound to the retained item's
linked source and is not a new discovery requirement. Held or unrelated bindings
cannot supply guidance. Actual replacement prose is newly generated from target facts. Record
the exact mentor excerpt, borrowed operation, target application and nontransferred
facts beside its private attribution. Distinguish mentor text, analytical explanation
and newly drafted target text. Unrelated sources and withdrawn suggestions are not
grounds for adding requirements during drafting.

Historical raw pages and private source mappings remain private. A minimized
package may preserve separately labeled literal excerpts; anonymization alone
does not establish disclosure permission. An abstraction-only package cannot
claim direct access to the mentor's exact prose. The source snapshot and
protocol are frozen with each run; old runs retain their original contracts.
