# Learning and applying editorial distinctions

For concrete mentor wording and syntax, follow
[expression-realization.md](expression-realization.md). Structural borrowing and
language-form borrowing are separately traceable: a task-before-obstacle operation
does not by itself demonstrate adoption of the source sentence form. Attribute only
features actually retained or adapted in the delivered target prose.

Use the original target as the baseline for both mentor and good-paper transfers. A valid source operation may be unnecessary in an already adequate target; keep/not_applicable is a valid runtime disposition, not failed coverage or an author-certified training example. A useful optional change needs a specific net reader benefit after transition, redundancy, emphasis and space costs. Equivalent style alone does not warrant author action. Compare competing operations for the same reader task and carry one compatible preferred path, preserving independently justified information requirements. Never infer preference or adoption merely from a final version or publication status.

Use this module in Reader, independent Advisor, and post-freeze revision drafting.
Learning here means retaining source-grounded distinctions and using them in a new
task. It does not update model weights. A longer checklist or additional concern
count alone does not establish that a writing practice was learned.

## What to learn from an example

Read the supplied passage account and its context, not merely its topic. Identify:
what the reader needs at entry; the job of each sentence/paragraph; the dependency
or contrast connecting them; what the reader can establish at exit; and which
conditions and qualifications make that sequence work. Preserve the relevant
argument unit, including a preceding question and following use where needed.

For example, a method overview may connect a resource constraint, exploitable
shared structure, an implementation obstacle, and the roles of successive stages.
The reusable distinction is how the mechanism answers the stated difficulty.
Neither that paper's algorithm nor its sentence count transfers to the new paper.
Test the target's own mechanism and existing explanation, including equations and
captions, before suggesting a bridge, reordered overview or coordinated paragraph.

The public case library supplies analyst reconstructions, including sentence roles
and contextual information. Its withheld `writing_pattern` fields remain unvalidated
research notes. Do not reintroduce them as ready-made rules. Develop an explicit
target-conditioned editorial hypothesis from the supplied source account instead.
An exact `source_excerpt` in the runtime record quotes that analytical account,
not the underlying paper or a mentor. Consult original source evidence when the
task requires validating or promoting that interpretation.

When an explicit private source-passage snapshot is supplied, a case additionally
contains separately labelled original anchor/neighbor-page text. An excerpt may
then quote that actual text; identify this basis in the transfer account. It does
so with `excerpt_basis="original_page_text"`; use `analyst_summary` for reconstructed
case accounts. The selected layer must actually contain the exact excerpt. This
does not certify extraction glyphs or make the source a mentor preference. An explicit
private Expression snapshot supplies actual attributed mentor proposals and full
original context through [expression.md](expression.md), independently of Concern.
Preserve partial proposals and unknown applied versions. Compare their conditional
sentence/paragraph operation with target facts rather than copying source facts.

## What counts as a mentor preference

A concern is a conditional question, not a preferred replacement sentence. Apply
all six decision fields and their evidence status. Distinguish:

- A manuscript-local requirement explicitly expressed by an identified mentor.
- A proposed change with before/after context and known or unknown authorship.
- A student response, including what it addresses and what later feedback changes.
- An analyst's explanation of a possible reader benefit or transferable practice.
- A pattern supported across distinct source families, with exceptions and conflict.

Public concern summaries alone cannot establish the original wording, the author's
identity or mentor acceptance. Silence, publication, a later revision, and repeated
versions of one paper cannot establish a stable preference. Do not turn uncertainty
into a permanent ban on learning: retain a provisional, narrowly scoped distinction
and state exactly which evidence is missing. A preference hypothesis may guide an
optional proposal if the target-specific gain and preserved facts support it.

When explicitly learning from historical files, use historical-training-intake.md
and [editorial-learning.md](editorial-learning.md). Preserve a private before/context/
feedback/after/follow-up record, the specific changed relationship, reasonable
alternatives and failure conditions. Classify local corrections, conditional
preferences and broader hypotheses separately. Never collapse all feedback to
"make it clearer" or infer a generic preference for shorter prose from one deletion.

## Apply at the passage's actual scale

Reader reuses its existing section/paragraph map. Independent Advisor reconstructs
the relevant passage job directly from the full manuscript without receiving that
map or a preliminary task inventory. For each applicable source,
compare the current passage with a concrete editorial proposal. Address selection
and emphasis as well as order: what earns its space, what repeats a job, what is
underexplained, whether the paragraph lands on its intended point, and how it hands
that point to the next paragraph. Include method, model, proof and experiment prose.
Do not stop at a local missing definition when the surrounding paragraph has a
separate, evidenced information-selection or argument-structure issue.

Specify the actual spans to retain, compress, split, merge, move, bridge or rewrite;
the reader's expected gain; necessary conditions, references and qualifications to
preserve; and the cost or alternative supported arrangement. An optional improvement
need not fix a technical error. Conversely, being technically correct or locally
understandable does not settle whether emphasis and paragraph progression work.

Use `writing_transfers` for source-to-target reasoning summaries. Keep genuine
independent actions distinct even when their paragraph or topic is shared. A single
coordinated rewrite may implement several linked actions. Do not inflate counts by
splitting synonyms, manufacture an alternative per case, or demand an edit to every
paragraph. Keep, unknown and unfinished outcomes are legitimate and distinguishable.

Advisor adds `writing_scope` to its existing card dispositions. Judge it by the
card's full relationship, including explanation and emphasis, not a keyword list.
An unknown historical record is not automatically a content-only concern. Advisor
still sees its own full manuscript and card batch, never Reader's plan or decisions.
Generic runs use the same editorial reasoning without access to historical sources.

For Advisor protocols with `advisor_role_division: 1`, Concern supplies the
requirement judgment; Expression supplies a concrete additional realization or
expression comparison. Record `expression_contribution` with its role, adopted
source feature IDs and genuinely supporting Concern IDs. Explain the contribution
in the existing transfer fields. A general Concern restated as an Expression is
not a second defect. Coordinate compatible actions while preserving each source
identity and independently justified requirement. Unlinked Expressions can support
optional improvements or target-evidenced local expression repairs; no forced
Concern association or rewrite is required. Post-freeze drafting retains these
records but must verify what was actually adopted in the final text.

## Preserve learning through delivery

Freeze initial transfers with the raw responses. Merge/Verify still judge target
evidence, without treating the mentor/source as authority. Follow both finding and
optional-suggestion dispositions, including optional-to-required incorporation.
The `writing_evidence.json` drafting packet carries the connected raw requirements,
checks, complete selected Reader source accounts and relevant six-field concerns.
It never turns a discarded suggestion into a retained requirement.

Draft from checked target facts. For paragraph-level work, state the new paragraph
roles and deliver supported prose at that scope, or an exact action-only repair when
moving/deleting already closes the issue. When facts are missing, identify them and
draft any independently supported portion. Cite source influence privately through
`revision_attribution`, bound to an actual report excerpt and target support.
Target-only drafting is valid. Do not add a source citation to make a suggestion
sound personalized. A legacy object-level case association is weaker than a recorded
transfer, and neither proves causal improvement. Attribute new drafting to its own
stage, never retroactively to an older review.

## Evaluate the intended improvement

Evaluate coverage and precision of independently actionable editorial requirements,
source-grounded specificity, preservation of target meaning, supported paragraph
repairs, and unsupported preference/defect claims. Finding counts alone are not a
quality score. Use actual held-out source families for transfer claims; known drafts
remain development examples. Keep model, effort, tier, manuscript and generic Reader
rules constant when comparing source use; compare dev.3 against dev.4 separately as
a whole-system comparison. Freeze each output before showing mentor feedback to the
evaluator. A local schema check demonstrates wiring, not better prose or mentor fit.
