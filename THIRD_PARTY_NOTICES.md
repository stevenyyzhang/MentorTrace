# Distribution and source boundaries

No third-party program source, executable, font, manuscript PDF or full-page article transcription is bundled. Python, pypdf, lxml, Pandoc, Chrome/Chromium/Edge, Poppler and Codex CLI are installed separately and retain their respective licenses.

The published-paper case records are MentorTrace analyst-written summaries with DOI/source links. They are not author endorsements or independent proofs of the papers' mathematics. Article titles and bibliographic references identify public sources; no private corpus identities or local PDF locations are distributed. A publicly accessible article is not automatically licensed for republication of its full text.

The Advisor records are anonymized conditional analytical abstractions. Private annotations, marked passages, neighboring text, unpublished manuscript versions and private source mappings are not redistributed. Historical endorsement and unresolved evidence remain distinct from analyst interpretations.

The project software, skill instructions and documentation use MIT (`LICENSE`). Original public analytical records use CC BY 4.0 (`LICENSE-CORPUS.md`). These grants do not relicense third-party source papers, private feedback or excluded materials.
